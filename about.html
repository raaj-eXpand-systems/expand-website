About eXpand Systems

Two Principals. One Firm.

Technology leadership and financial expertise — built to work together, not sold separately.

Raaj Sharma

Technology Principal · We eliminate technology chaos before it becomes expensive.

That sounds simple. It isn't. Enterprise integration is where projects go to die — where vendor promises don't hold, APIs have undocumented edge cases, business requirements change mid-sprint, and the legacy system no one wanted to touch becomes the critical path.

I've been in every one of those rooms. And I've shipped anyway.

My career spans six companies across four countries — from integrating airline booking data in Gurgaon to architecting a complete member technology ecosystem for a global cybersecurity certification body with hundreds of thousands of members worldwide. Along the way I've led teams spanning Frontend Engineers, Backend Developers, DBAs, QA, Build Engineers, and Integration Specialists — managed budgets up to $8M, negotiated contracts that saved millions, and built integrations that most architects have never heard of, including one of the only live implementations of Pearson VUE's RTI/RTEN API outside of Pearson itself.

Before founding eXpand Systems, I served as Director of Business Applications and QA at a global cybersecurity certification body, where I built its technology ecosystem: Salesforce as the golden record and identity hub, MuleSoft as the integration layer, Sitecore XMC as the headless CMS, CommerceTools for eCommerce, Vertex for tax, Intacct for finance, and SSO integrations connecting the platforms into a single member experience.

  • Machine Learning Certificate
  • SQL Server DBA certification (foundational database architecture expertise)
  • Master of Computer Applications
  • 26 years continuous enterprise technology experience
