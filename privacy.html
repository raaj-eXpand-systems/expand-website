Your information stays purposeful.

Information you provide

When you submit our contact form, we receive the name, email address, organization, service interest, and message you choose to provide. We use this information only to review and respond to your inquiry, discuss a potential engagement, and maintain appropriate business records.

Service providers and external links

Contact-form submissions are processed by Formspree. Scheduling is provided through Calendly when you follow the scheduling link. This website also loads fonts from Google Fonts. These providers may process technical information under their own privacy terms. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention and protection

We retain inquiry information only as long as reasonably needed for communication, business administration, legal obligations, and dispute prevention. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete information you submitted, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements. Use the contact form and state that your message concerns privacy.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as our website or service providers change. The date above identifies the latest version.
