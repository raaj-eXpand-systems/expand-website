Narratrace

Family stories deserve more than a camera roll.

Narratrace is a private digital home for preserving family memories, photographs, voices, recipes, letters, and meaningful moments across generations.

We designed and built Narratrace end-to-end—from its memory experience and family relationships to privacy controls, secure delivery, subscriptions, administration, and mobile-ready architecture.

  • Preserve naturallyCapture memories without turning family history into a filing project.
  • Share privatelyKeep meaningful stories within the family circles chosen by their owners.
  • Deliver meaningfullySend memories now or at a carefully chosen future moment.
  • Use AI thoughtfullyHelp people tell richer stories while keeping human memory at the center.
